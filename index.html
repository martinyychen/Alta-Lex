04TABULAR REVIEW

Batch review hundreds of documents in one table.

Every document becomes a row; every review criterion becomes a column. Tabular Review reads each contract, filing or exhibit in a folder, answers the same questions for every file and lays the results side by side — each cell cited back to the passage it came from.

Where it earns its place
M&A DUE DILIGENCE“Which contracts need consent on a change of control?”
Every material contract in the data room, read for the same terms — then sorted by risk.
COMPLIANCE INVESTIGATIONS“Where do the approvals not match the policy?”
The same questions put to thousands of emails, policies and approvals; the outliers surface on their own.
LITIGATION & ARBITRATION“What did each witness admit, and on which page?”
Facts pulled from the whole case file, each cell cited back to its source page.
How it works
01Choose a folderOpen a blank chat, switch on Tabular Review and add a Library or Project folder.
02Ask in the chatDescribe what to review in the chat — then send.
03Work the tableCompare answers side by side and open the cited passage behind any cell.
